Welcome to Those Cursed Treasures

The Quest for El Dorado

A homebrewed expansion for Reiner Knizia's brilliant racing deck-builder, designed to be played with the base game using the cave variant.

To play, add each player, then use the Draw Treasure button on a player's card when they discover a treasure. Every treasure brings a curse!

Extra things needed: 2 tokens per board to represent Cursed Treasures, and 1 token to represent the Shrine.
Round: 1

💰 Every Treasure Is Cursed

Risk meets reward. Draw a treasure? You’re cursed—no exceptions.

🎒 Getting Started

⚙️ Setup

  • Set up a game as you would normally.
  • Place 2 Cursed Treasures (any token that fits on a hex) per board used in your map construction.
  • Place treasures randomly or on hexes further from the most direct route to enhance their impact and temptation.

🛠️ How It Works

  • Approach a treasure on the map and, if you are not cursed, draw a treasure.
  • Remove the treasure token from the board.
  • Drawing a treasure always gives you a curse.
  • Curses take effect immediately and stay until cleansed.
  • You can carry only one curse at a time.

🧼 Cleansing Ritual

  • Pay the curse’s cleanse cost from your stash to perform a cleansing ritual. On Normal, costs are randomly assigned in a 2:2:1 split of 2, 3, and 4 gold.
  • Cleansing removes the curse and grants a Cleansing Benefit. Benefits are specific to the curse and scale with its severity and cleanse cost.
  • Benefits do not stack. A new Cleansing Benefit replaces the previous one unless it has already been satisfied.
  • Once cleansed, you may draw treasures again.
  • Gold can be stashed on your turn to save for a ritual or Purge.
  • As with card purchases, non-yellow cards count as half a coin when stashing gold.

⛩️ Shrines

  • After 2–12 treasures have been drawn, a Shrine may appear at random.
  • A cursed player can use the Shrine to cleanse their curse for free and still gain the cleanse benefit.
  • Only one player may use the Shrine per appearance.
  • The active player places the Shrine token on a hex exactly the specified number of hexes away from their piece. Any unique token that fits on a hex will work.
  • The player may choose any hex at that exact distance, making the Shrine easier or harder to reach.
  • Approach the Shrine like a Treasure, then select Use Shrine!

✨ Advanced Actions

💥 Purge

You may choose to double the cleanse cost of your curse to perform a Purge.

Purge removes your curse and grants both:

  1. The normal cleansing benefit of the curse.
  2. A powerful random, stackable Purge Perk, such as:
  • Reduce future card purchase costs by 1 gold.
  • Gain immunity to your next curse.
  • Automatically discover the nearest treasure without movement.

Purge is expensive, but its rewards can be game-changing and accumulative.

🎯 Throw Curse

  • If you are cursed, you may attempt to throw your curse at another player.
  • Pay half of your curse’s cleanse cost to attempt it.
  • Select any player who does not already have a curse.
  • There is a 50% chance:
    • 🎯 Success: They immediately receive your curse, but no treasure.
    • ❌ Backfire: You lose your money, keep the curse, and its cleanse cost doubles.

This adds high-risk, high-reward player interaction and tense decisions.

🔥 Pro Tip

Curses sting—but their cleansing perks can flip the game. Spend smart, cleanse wisely, and take bold risks!

📈 Scalable Cleanse Benefits

  • Curses offer greater rewards based on their cleanse cost.
  • A higher cleanse cost means a greater benefit when the curse is removed. This still applies if a failed Throw Curse action doubles the cost (for example, a cost of 8 or more gives especially powerful benefits).

🏆 Choose an Endgame

How should treasures affect the endgame? That’s up to you!

💀 Classic Cursed

Race to El Dorado as usual, but collected treasures offset the turns taken to reach the end.

For example, Sol reaches the end in 10 rounds with 3 treasures, while Nadine reaches it in 12 rounds with 6 treasures. Nadine wins.

  • Sol: 10 rounds − 3 treasures = 7
  • Nadine: 12 rounds − 6 treasures = 6 ✅

🏴‍☠️ Race to the Hoard

Race around the map like greedy jungle pillagers to collect and cleanse a set number of treasures:

  • 2 players: 6–7 treasures
  • 3–4 players: 5 treasures
